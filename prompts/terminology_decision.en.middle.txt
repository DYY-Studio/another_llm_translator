Curate the terminology library using actual occurrence evidence from the complete work. hit_count is the number of Segments matching the term, not the total number of substring occurrences. The samples first include first hits from different files, then fill from other distinct Segments. Prioritize consistent translations for people, places, organizations, proper names, and recurring concepts. Protected human anchors, automatic anchors whose phase-one disposition is determined and currently conflict-free, and explicit full-name, short-form, or nickname relationships take precedence over general language habits.

Fill missing translations for terms worth translating and resolve category and translation conflicts. Deduplicated category, translation, and relationship candidates in conflicts are historical evidence, not votes or an allowed-value whitelist; propose a new value outside those candidates when the work-wide evidence supports it. Reuse category wording already used by related terms and anchors when reliable; do not invent a closed category enum. A conflict-free term may keep its value when evidence is insufficient, but a conflicted term that cannot be resolved reliably must use needs_review. Use the target language's ordinary conventions and the work's existing anchors for translations; do not assume a universal transliteration table. Use the same translated surname across related full names, and make full names, nicknames, pet names, and abbreviations visibly related. Apply this reasoning to Japanese kanji and kana, katakana-rendered Western names, and ordinary Western names.

Remove aliases that never occur, duplicate aliases, and aliases that do not denote the same term. Receiving another term's existing source or alias must be a complete multi-term transfer: the receiver is an enabled root, while the previous owner releases the alias, is disabled, or (for a source form) becomes a direct member of the receiver. Never steal a form in only one decision. Keep a description only when it materially disambiguates the term. You may rewrite redundant or contradictory text as a concise target-language description when the current description, source samples, or visible anchors support it; otherwise clear it. Never add facts absent from that evidence.

A zero hit may support disabling only after checking anchors, group relations, and story importance. A single hit may support disable only when its context clearly shows an incidental ordinary word that is not a person, proper name, title, or plot concept; one hit alone is never a removal rule. In phase two, update or disable only when cross-term evidence requires correcting the phase-one provisional state; otherwise keep.

Never modify source or normalized, invent a source alias, or alter anchors. Use needs_review instead of guessing or when a complete relationship component cannot be represented.
